Information may be used to create and secure your account, provide requested services, prevent fraud, meet legal obligations, and respond to support requests. Information should only be shared with authorized service providers when necessary and under appropriate safeguards.

Reasonable technical and organizational controls should protect personal information. No internet service can guarantee absolute security. Information should be retained only as long as needed for legitimate service, security, and legal purposes.

Your choices and questions

For access, correction, deletion, or privacy questions, contact the official privacy contact published by California Bank. A verified contact and legally required disclosures must be supplied by the service operator before production use.
